01 — About

A bit about me.

I'm Mohamed Amine SALHI — a Business Intelligence student at ISIGK, working at the intersection of full-stack web development, data automation, and native desktop software. I like problems that force me to learn four new things at once.

I build cross-platform desktop apps with Tauri + Rust and native Qt6 + C++, and full-stack web products with React, Node.js, and TypeScript. If it runs fast, looks clean, and replaces a tedious paper process — I'm interested.

My graduation project, BK-Prod, is a real-time production traceability platform now used daily at a construction company — replacing paper logs with a live digital system. Right now I'm learning RAG pipelines and AI automation, and shipping VOID on the side.

⚡ Fun fact: I use Arch Linux (CachyOS) btw.
